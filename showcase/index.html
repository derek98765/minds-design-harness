Color

Two brand colors do all the work: orange for action, indigo for structure. Orange is rationed so strictly it reads as instruction rather than decoration — it is never a surface color and never a heading color.

Orange — the action color

Blue — structure, eyebrows, links, inverted canvas

Beige — the warm canvas family

Neutral — text and borders

Semantic roles — prefer these in markup

Rule. Adjacent sections never share a background. Warm canvas → white → warm canvas is the default rhythm. On the indigo canvas, headings go light, body goes lighter, and the CTA stays orange.

Typography

Manrope only. Extrabold or quiet, nothing between — headlines sit at 800, body at 400, and the gap between them is the hierarchy. Tracking runs opposite by scale: headlines tighten, eyebrows and captions open up. Every size below is responsive already.

Don't. No all-caps headlines. No second font family. No serif display face. Headlines may highlight exactly one phrase in indigo — never in orange.

Spacing

Numbers are literal pixels, not Tailwind's 4× scale. These 21 values are the only valid ones. Anything else fails silently — Tailwind resolves it against its built-in scale, so the layout breaks without throwing an error.

The silent failure, made visible

When in doubt, round down. Use py-8 not py-10, gap-12 not gap-14.

Radius & depth

The shape system is uniformly soft — every button is a pill, nothing is square. Depth is nearly absent: separation comes from background change and whitespace, and shadows appear only where something genuinely floats.
