# Logo

Read this when a page has a header, footer, or any logo placement.

Files are in `assets/logo/`:

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `minds-blue.svg` | Default. On the warm canvas, on white, on any light surface. |
| `minds-white.svg` | On the indigo canvas, on photography, on any dark surface. |
| `minds-blue.png` / `minds-white.png` | Only where SVG is not an option (email, some social). |

Prefer the SVG everywhere on the web — it stays sharp at every size.

---

## Minimum sizes

| Context | Minimum | Below this |
|---|---|---|
| Digital | **120px wide** | Use the icon mark alone, no wordmark |
| Print | **30mm wide** | — |
| Favicon / app icon | **32×32px** | Icon mark only, never the wordmark |

Below 120px the "By Animoca Brands" line becomes illegible, which is worse than
dropping it. Use the mark on its own instead of shrinking the full lockup.

---

## Clear space

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "M" in the wordmark on
every side. Nothing enters that box — no text, no button, no image edge, no other logo.

---

## Optical centering

**Shift the logo slightly left of true geometric center.** Its visual weight sits on
the left (the icon mark), so mathematically centering it creates the illusion of
excess space on the right. Adjust for perceived balance, not measured balance.

This matters most in a centered footer or a splash screen.

---

## Contrast

Always place the logo on a background with strong contrast. The safe pairings:

- Blue logo on warm canvas, white, or any pale surface
- White logo on the indigo canvas or on a dark photograph

**Never place the blue logo on orange** — the contrast is too low and it is a
documented don't.

---

## The eight don'ts

Every one of these is explicitly prohibited in the brand guideline:

1. **Mathematical centering** — use optical centering instead (see above)
2. **Stretching or distorting** — always scale proportionally
3. **Changing the font or letterforms** — the wordmark is fixed artwork, never retype it
4. **Effects** — no drop shadows, outlines, glows, or gradients on the logo
5. **Rotation** — the logo sits level, always
6. **Unapproved color combinations** — blue or white only, never recolored
7. **Rearranging or separating wordmark elements** — the mark and wordmark keep their relationship
8. **Low-contrast backgrounds** — including blue-on-orange

---

## Variants

Beyond the primary logo, the guideline defines:

- **Dream Minds** — a sub-brand lockup, used for that product only
- **Collaborated variants** — partner lockups (e.g. NEOM Minds, HelpBnk Dream Minds)

Do not create a new collaborated variant yourself. If a campaign needs a partner
lockup, that is a design request, not something to assemble from parts.

---

## In copy

The logo is artwork. In text, the brand is written **"Minds"** — never "Animoca
Minds". The "By Animoca Brands" line exists only inside the logo file itself. See
[voice-and-copy.md](voice-and-copy.md).
