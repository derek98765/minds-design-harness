Minds Style Guide

Every token in ui/tokens.css and every component in ui/react/, on one page. Tokens come in two layers. BASE COLORS are raw ramps numbered by lightness (50 near-white → 900 darkest), saying only how light. SEMANTIC TOKENS say what for, each pointing at one base step, e.g. --blue-lit → var(--blue-600). Pages use the semantic layer only — retune a role by repointing its token, never by editing a shared ramp step. Click a swatch to copy its var(--name).

Type and Layout

Typography Manrope · sizes as this screen renders them

Marketing scale for landing pages and campaign heroes; product scale for app surfaces. Both step down at 1024px and 767px. Headlines are 800 and track tight. Body copy is cool grey, not black. At most one highlighted phrase per headline, in --blue-lit.

Spacing 21 steps · literal pixels

These are the only valid spacing numbers, and they are pixels: gap-8 is 8px, not 32px. Any other number silently falls back to Tailwind's 4px scale — py-10 renders 40px with no error. When a value falls between steps, round down.

Radius

Pill for buttons, chips and avatars. --r-xl for large glass shells, --r-lg for cards, --r-md for inputs and images, --r-sm for micro surfaces.

Elevation

Drops are blue-grey, not neutral — a grey drop under glass reads as dirt. Do not invent a second shadow to separate sections. --shadow-lit-* belongs only on its matching lit button (see Buttons above).

Logo assets/brand/logo/

Blue on the wash, white, or glass; white on photography and dark surfaces. Below 120px wide, use the mark alone. Never retype the wordmark, recolour it, or put the blue logo on orange. Rules: spec/brand-logo.md.

Input, Textarea, Labelinput.jsx · textarea.jsx · label.jsx

Input is a bare field — no fill, no border — meant to sit inside a field shell that the page draws: --bg-field-cool, a --line-cool hairline, --r-md (pill for search), and a 1px --blue ring on focus. Fields render at 16px or more on mobile so iOS Safari doesn't zoom.

Tabstabs.jsx

Two shapes on one Radix root. Pill tabs switch a card's or panel's content. Underline tabs are page or section navigation — sm for a secondary row, lg when the tabs must hold their own next to a headline. The current tab is --blue-lit, never orange. Don't mix shapes in one group.
