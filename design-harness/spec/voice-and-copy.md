# Voice & copy

Read this before writing or rewriting any headline or body copy.

For a marketing page, copy is most of what makes it feel like Minds. A page can use
every token correctly and still read as generic AI software.

---

## The name

**The brand is "Minds."** Never "Animoca Minds" in a headline, sentence, or UI label.
The lockup "By Animoca Brands" lives inside the logo artwork only — never retype it
as text.

A single agent is **"a Mind."** Several are **"Minds."** Capitalised, always — it is
a product name, not a common noun. "Your Mind books the table," not "your mind books
the table."

Never translate "Minds", "AWS", "AFC Virtual League" or other brand terms into another
language, even mid-sentence.

---

## The core rule: concrete outcomes, not capabilities

Almost every weak line on a Minds page fails the same way — it describes what the
software *is* rather than what the reader *gets*.

| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| Leverage AI-powered task automation | Handle bookings, reminders, and daily life tasks |
| Advanced persistent memory architecture | It remembers how you work, so you don't start over |
| Seamless multi-channel integration | Talk to it in email or Telegram, like a colleague |
| Optimise your productivity workflow | Get six hours back a week |
| Next-generation agentic capabilities | It works while you sleep |

The test: could a competitor put the same sentence on their page? If yes, it says
nothing. Rewrite it with a specific noun — a calendar, an inbox, an Etsy listing, a
Tuesday.

---

## Headline patterns that work

Drawn from the brand guideline and shipped pages:

**Direct promise.** State the outcome as a plain fact.
> Works while you sleep
> It learns your workflow. It does the heavy lifting.

**Question the reader is already asking.**
> What your Mind can do

**Two-beat sentence.** A short claim, then the consequence.
> Your Mind handles it, while you get on with the day

**Named job.** For a specific Mind, name the job and the deliverable.
> Turns your tasks and meetings into one clear, moving plan
> Reach inbox zero and keep it there with smart cleanup

### Rules for headlines
- **Never all caps.** Eyebrows are uppercase; headlines never are.
- One phrase may be highlighted in indigo. One — never two, never in orange.
- Sentence case or title case, consistently within a page.
- Short enough to read in one breath. If it needs a comma and a semicolon, it's two headlines.

---

## Body copy

- **One idea per paragraph.** Two or three sentences at most.
- **Plain words.** "Use", not "utilise". "Help", not "facilitate". "About", not "approximately".
- **Second person.** "You", "your Mind" — not "users" or "customers".
- **Active voice.** "Your Mind drafts the reply", not "replies are drafted".
- Vary sentence length. A short one lands harder after a longer one.
- Cut the first sentence of any paragraph that warms up before saying something.

---

## Eyebrows

Uppercase, medium weight, indigo, two to four words. They orient, they don't sell.

> ONE-CLICK MINDS · WHAT YOUR MIND CAN DO · QUESTIONS · HOW IT WORKS

Not a sentence, no full stop, no exclamation.

---

## Buttons and CTAs

- Start with a verb: "Create your Mind", "Get started free", "See how it works".
- Say what happens next, not "Submit" or "Click here".
- Reassure right below the button where there's friction: "Free to try. No card, no setup call."

---

## What Minds never says

- **No hype adjectives.** revolutionary, cutting-edge, game-changing, seamless, effortless, powerful, robust.
- **No exclamation marks** in headlines or body. The type carries emphasis.
- **No fake urgency.** "Act now", "Don't miss out", countdown pressure on an evergreen page.
- **No jargon as a selling point.** Agentic, LLM, RAG, multi-modal — a busy parent doesn't care.
- **No apologising or hedging.** "We think you might find..." — say it or cut it.
- **No emoji in headlines or body copy.**

---

## Tone check

Read the page aloud. It should sound like a capable colleague explaining something
useful over coffee — warm, direct, unhurried, never performing.

If it sounds like a press release, a pitch deck, or a chatbot, rewrite it.

---

## Worked example

**Before**
> Revolutionise Your Workflow with AI!
> Our cutting-edge agentic platform leverages advanced persistent memory to
> seamlessly optimise your daily productivity across multiple integrated channels.

Everything wrong at once: all-caps-adjacent hype, an exclamation mark, five abstract
nouns in a row, no specific thing the reader gets, and a competitor could publish it
verbatim.

**After**
> ONE-CLICK MINDS
> ## Your Mind handles it, *while you get on with the day*
> Set it up once through email or the apps you already use. No prompt engineering,
> no dashboard to learn.
> **[Create your Mind]**  Free to try. No card, no setup call.

Concrete, second person, one indigo highlight, one orange action, and the objection
handled under the button.
